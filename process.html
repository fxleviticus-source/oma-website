Our Process

A clear, orbiting path from first conversation to launch.

Every OMA project moves through the same six stages — giving you a clear sense of where things stand at every point.

Ideas → Strategy → Creation → Growth

One continuous cycle, not a hand-off chain.

Each stage of an OMA project feeds the next — discovery shapes strategy, strategy shapes design, and what we learn at launch feeds back into how the business keeps growing. The process below is how that cycle plays out on every project, from a single landing page to a full multi-page website.

01

Discovery

We start with conversation — understanding your business, your customers, your competitors and what success actually looks like for this project.

02

Strategy

We translate discovery into a plan: site structure, key messages, and the priorities that will shape every design decision after this point.

03

Design

We design the visual direction and user experience — layouts, typography, color and imagery that reflect your brand and guide visitors toward action.

04

Development

We build the responsive website, coding every page to match the approved design and to perform well across devices and browsers.

05

Testing

We test across devices, screen sizes and browsers, checking links, forms, animations and performance before anything goes live.

06

Launch

We launch the finished project and stay involved afterward — ready to support updates and improvements as your business grows.
